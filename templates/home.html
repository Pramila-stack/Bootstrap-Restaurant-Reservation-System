Our Story

Crafted With
Passion, Served
With Pride

Founded in 2018, Maison Rouge was born from a singular obsession — to create a dining experience that transcends the ordinary. Our head chef trained under three Michelin-starred masters across Paris, Tokyo, and New York.

Every detail, from the hand-folded napkins to the last drizzle of truffle oil, is an act of devotion to the craft of hospitality.

  • House-made pasta & breads baked fresh daily
  • 70% of ingredients sourced within 150km
  • Private dining rooms for groups up to 20
  • Custom tasting menus with 48 hours notice
  • Award-winning wine cellar of 200+ labels
